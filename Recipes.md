# High Protein Alfredo Banza Pasta

## Ingredients

- 2 boxes Banza pasta
    
- 3 lbs 93/7 ground turkey
    
- 3 cups fat free Fairlife milk
    
- 200g reduced fat cream cheese
    
- 1 bag frozen vegetables (optional)
    
- Garlic powder
    
- Onion powder
    
- Salt
    
- Black pepper
    

---

## Instructions

### 1. Cook Pasta

- Boil Banza according to package directions.
    
- Drain and set aside.
    

### 2. Cook Turkey

- Brown turkey in a large pan.
    
- Season with:
    
    - garlic powder
        
    - onion powder
        
    - salt
        
    - black pepper
        

### 3. Add Vegetables (Optional)

- Add frozen vegetables directly into the turkey mixture.
    
- Cook until heated through.
    

### 4. Make Alfredo Sauce

In a separate pot over LOW heat:

- Add Fairlife milk
    
- Add reduced fat cream cheese
    

Whisk until smooth.

Season with:

- garlic powder
    
- black pepper
    
- salt
    

Do not boil aggressively.

### 5. Combine

- Add pasta to turkey.
    
- Pour sauce over mixture.
    
- Stir thoroughly.
    

### 6. Portion

Split into 7 containers.

---

## Approximate Macros (Per Meal)

|Macro|Amount|
|---|---|
|Calories|~550|
|Protein|~60g|
|Carbs|~43–44g|
|Fat|~16g|

# Fast Breakfast Burrito

## Ingredients (1 Burrito)

- 2 whole eggs
    
- 300g liquid egg whites
    
- 1 large flour tortilla
    
- 28g reduced fat shredded cheese
    

Optional:

- salsa
    
- hot sauce
    
- black pepper
    

---

## Instructions

### 1. Cook Eggs

- Scramble eggs and egg whites together.
    
- Season as desired.
    

### 2. Assemble

- Add cheese to tortilla.
    
- Add eggs.
    
- Add optional salsa or hot sauce.
    

### 3. Optional

- Pan sear briefly for texture.
    

---

## Approximate Macros

|Macro|Amount|
|---|---|
|Calories|~500–600|
|Protein|~50–52g|
|Carbs|~40–45g|
|Fat|~14–16g|
