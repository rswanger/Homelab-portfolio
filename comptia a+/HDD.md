That classic:

> clicking / ticking / mechanical knocking

sound is usually associated with a failing HDD because it has:

- moving platters
- moving read/write heads

SSDs don’t have moving parts, so they typically fail silently instead of mechanically.

This is also a good real-world troubleshooting clue:  
unusual mechanical noises often point toward:

- HDD failure
- fan issues
- sometimes PSU/fan bearings
